# FAQ

??? question "The mod isn't showing up in game. What do I do?"
    Make sure it's enabled in your mod list, and that all of its dependencies are installed too.

??? question "Can I use these mods on my server?"
    Yes. See the **Server setup** section on each mod's page.

??? question "Where do I report a bug?"
    Let us know on our community channels, and include the mod's version number and what you were doing when it happened.
